Before you come

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are seen in the order they arrive. Appointments are advised for dental, prenatal and laboratory visits to shorten your wait.

What should I bring?

A valid ID, your health insurance card if you have one, and any previous prescriptions or laboratory results.

Which insurance do you accept?

We accept PhilHealth and most major HMO cards. Bring your member details so the front desk can confirm coverage.

How do I prepare for a fasting blood test?

Do not eat or drink anything except water for 8 to 10 hours before your sample. Take your regular medicines unless your doctor tells you otherwise.

When should I go to the emergency room instead?

Go straight to the emergency room for chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, sudden weakness, seizures or serious injury. It is open 24 hours.

Privacy notice

Appointment and health information is sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). We collect only the details you choose to provide to prepare for your consultation.

Who can see it: You can see your own appointment. Only authorized clinic staff signed in with an administrator account can review intake details. Intake details are not public and are not sent by email.

How long it is kept: The clinic has not provided an approved fixed retention period. Until the clinic confirms one, this notice cannot state a specific duration. Information should be retained only as long as needed for care and applicable record-keeping requirements, then securely deleted or de-identified. Clinic/privacy-officer review is required before using this form with real patients.

Access, correction, or deletion: Contact the clinic using verified contact information to request access to or correction/deletion of your information. Some records may need to be kept when required by applicable obligations. A privacy contact has not yet been provided for this site and must be confirmed before launch.

This notice is a draft for clinic review, not legal advice. The contact details elsewhere on this demonstration site are marked as sample content and should be verified before use.
