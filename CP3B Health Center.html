Before you come

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are seen in the order they arrive. Appointments are advised for dental, prenatal and laboratory visits to shorten your wait.

What should I bring?

A valid ID, your health insurance card if you have one, and any previous prescriptions or laboratory results.

Which insurance do you accept?

We accept PhilHealth and most major HMO cards. Bring your member details so the front desk can confirm coverage.

How do I prepare for a fasting blood test?

Do not eat or drink anything except water for 8 to 10 hours before your sample. Take your regular medicines unless your doctor tells you otherwise.

When should I go to the emergency room instead?

Go straight to the emergency room for chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, sudden weakness, seizures or serious injury. It is open 24 hours.
